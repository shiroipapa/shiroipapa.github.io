Privacy

Short, because there is little to describe.

This website

  • No cookies are set.
  • This site does not load third-party analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, or external fonts. The pages and images are served from this site's own repository.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub, as the host, handles the requests that deliver each page; what GitHub records is governed by GitHub's own privacy statement, which we do not control and do not restate here.
  • There is no contact form, so nothing is submitted to us from this site.

Email you send us

Messages sent to the support address are used to answer you and to fix the problem you reported. We do not use them for marketing and do not share them with anyone else. The mailbox is a Gmail account, so Google handles the mail itself.

Our products

  • LocaleProof contains no telemetry and makes no network calls. It reads the CSV files you point it at and writes only where you tell it to.
  • Products sold through a store are distributed by that store; the store handles the purchase and holds the customer record, not us.

If you need something clarified before a product goes on sale, ask at shiroipapaapps@gmail.com.
