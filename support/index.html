Questions about a product, bug reports, licence problems.

What to include in a bug report

  • The product and its version.
  • For Unity products: your Unity version and the version of the packages involved (for LocaleProof, com.unity.localization).
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • If the tool produced a report or a result screen, the numbers from it.
  • A small file that reproduces the problem, if you can share one. Please remove anything confidential first — a small test file is often more useful than a real one with parts removed in a hurry.

Product-specific help
